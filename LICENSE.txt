A NOTE ON WHAT THE RESULTS MEAN

A docking score is an estimate produced by a scoring function. It is not a
measured binding free energy and it does not reliably predict potency. Poses,
interactions, affinities and rankings reported by DawnDock are computational
predictions and can be wrong.

DawnDock is a research tool. It is not a medical device and is not for
diagnosis, treatment, or any clinical decision.
